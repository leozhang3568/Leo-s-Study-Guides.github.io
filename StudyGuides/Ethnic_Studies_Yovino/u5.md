A sociologist and civil rights activist who co-founded the National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP). | W.E.B. DuBois
Laws that enforced racial segregation and discrimination in the southern United States. | Jim Crow Laws
The New Negro Renaissance occurred in which following city? | Harlem
What is the Triangular Trade? | A trade route between Europe, Africa, and the Americas

Which industry provided employment opportunities for many African Americans in northern cities during the Great Migration? | Manufacturing and industrial sectors.
An educator and civil rights leader who advocated for vocational education and economic self-sufficiency for African Americans. | Booker T. Washington
What were the primary crops cultivated by enslaved people in the Southern United States? | Cotton and tobacco
An enslaved African who wrote a book about his experiences, which became a powerful argument against slavery. | Olaudah Equiano
This man advocated for Black Excellence and a return to Africa | Marcus Garvey

This holiday celebrates the emancipation of slaves in America | Juneteeth
