# Caesar Act III

&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; *Flourish. Enter Caesar, Brutus, Cassius, Cascaa, Decius, Metellus [Cimber], Trebonius, Cinna, Antony, Lepidus, Artemidorus, Publius, [Popilius Lena], and the Soothsayer; [others following].*

CAESAR [*to the soothsayer] &nbsp; &nbsp;The ides of March are come.

SOOTHSAYER &nbsp; &nbsp;Ay, Caesar, but not gone.

ARTEMIDORUS &nbsp; &nbsp;Hail, Caesar! Read this schedule.

DECIUS <br>
&nbsp; &nbsp; Trebonius doth desire you to o'erread, <br />
&nbsp; &nbsp; At your best leisure, this his humble suit.
